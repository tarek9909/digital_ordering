## 2. Before the first post

1. **Professional account**: switch @tanbeeh.lb to a *Business* account, link it to a Facebook Page and to Meta Business Suite. Ads and the message setup need this.
2. **WhatsApp Business** on the number that will answer leads. Link it to the Page (Business Suite → Settings → WhatsApp).
3. **Profile picture**: `identity/social/profile-avatar-1080.png`.
4. **Bio for the tease week** (until Sun 1 Nov):
   ```
   تنبيه | Tanbeeh
   قرّب يجهز. منخبّركن.
   Almost done. We'll tell you. 🟠
   ```
5. **Bio from launch day (Mon 2 Nov)**:
   ```
   تنبيه | Tanbeeh
   تلفون زبونك صار هو الجرس 🟠
   بلا تطبيق • بلا أجهزة • جاهز بأقل من ١٠ دقايق
   للمطاعم والمقاهي بلبنان 🇱🇧
   👇 احجز عرض · Book a demo
   ```
   Link: your WhatsApp click-to-chat link (`wa.me/961XXXXXXXX`) or the website.
6. **Messaging autoreplies** (Business Suite → Inbox → Automations). Ads send people to DMs, so this matters most:
   - **Instant reply**: `أهلا 👋 شكراً لتواصلك مع تنبيه! شو اسم محلّك وبأي منطقة؟ منرجعلك بأقل من ساعة. / Hi! What's your venue's name and area? We'll get back to you within the hour.`
   - **Frequently asked questions (max 4)**:
     1. `قدّيش بيكلّف؟ / How much is it?`
     2. `بدّي عرض لمحلّي / I want a demo`
     3. `لازم إشتري أجهزة؟ / Do I need to buy devices?`
     4. `بيشتغل مع نظام الكاشير؟ / Does it work with my POS?`
   - Have your answer to "how much" ready. The content says "priced in US dollars" but never shows a number, and that's the first thing people will ask.
7. **Highlights** to create in week 2: `كيف بيشتغل` (02, carousel, 07) · `لصاحب المحل` (22, 23, 28) · `أسئلة` (answers to DMs) · `Demo`. Covers: plain ember dot on charcoal.

### Posting rules for every post
- Upload the **videos as Reels** with the **original audio** (they're cut to it). Turn on "Also share to Facebook".
- **Reel cover**: pick the frame with the headline in Arabic (e.g. 02 → "بلا تطبيق. بس امسح.").
- **Location tag**: Beirut, Lebanon.
- After each feed post, **share it to your story** within 1 hour.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Early replies push reach on a new account.
- 3–6 hashtags per post, no more. Rotate from:
  `#تنبيه #Tanbeeh #مطاعم_لبنان #بيروت #Lebanon #Beirut #LebaneseFood #RestaurantOwner #CafeLebanon #FoodBusiness #RestaurantTech`

### When to post (why these times)
| Slot | Used for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **10:30** | Owner content | Owners are on their phones before the lunch rush |
| **16:30** | Owner posters | The afternoon lull between lunch and dinner |
| **20:00** | Relatable / problem content | Lebanon's general evening scroll peak |
| **23:00–23:30** | 24, 31 | After closing. Both videos happen at night, so the timing is the joke |
| **09:30 / 11:00 weekend** | Coffee / manoushe reels | Matches the food in the video |

## 4. Meta ads (small budget)

### Which video, and why

**Main ad: `05-ThirteenOrThirty.mp4`.** Most quick-service places in Lebanon don't own buzzers; they **shout numbers and names**. This video is that exact sound ("أبو علي!", "٢٧؟!") in the first second, so it stops the scroll for owners and staff alike. It's only 15 seconds and ends on the promise: "بلا صريخ. Stop shouting. Start alerting."

Run it against two challengers in the same ad set, and let Meta move the money to the winner:
- **`23-CounterAt1pm.mp4`**: speaks to money (lost orders at lunch).
- **`28-AlreadyOwnHardware.mp4`**: removes the biggest objection ("$0 new hardware").

For retargeting (people who already watched), use **`02-TwentySeven.mp4`** (the full 40s demo) and **`07-TenMinutes.mp4`**.

Not for ads: `21-BuzzerBill` (only fits venues that already own buzzers, a minority); the 5-second shorts (too short to explain anything).

### Running the ads
- **Answer DMs within an hour**, 10:00–22:00. A message ad that waits for a reply wastes its money.
- **Don't edit anything for the first 4 days** (the learning phase). Edits restart it.
- **Day 7 check** (Thu 12 Nov): pause any ad that has spent ~$10 with **zero conversations**, or has a link CTR under 0.8%. Keep the rest running.
- Watch **cost per conversation**. Lebanon is usually a cheap market, but set your own benchmark from the first week rather than trusting a number from elsewhere.
- Log every conversation (name, venue, area, answer to "how much?") in a sheet. What owners ask most tells you the next videos to make.
- Don't "Boost" posts from the app. Boost doesn't offer these settings.
